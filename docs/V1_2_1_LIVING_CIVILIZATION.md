# RedWorld AI v1.2.1 — Living Civilization

This release integrates the entire planned Living Civilization capability line into one final `v1.2.1` package.

## Integrated systems

1. Living Civilization Foundation
2. Life & Generations
3. Careers & Social Mobility
4. Dynamic Economy
5. Politics & Governance
6. Social Dynamics
7. City Evolution
8. Events & Crises
9. History & Causality
10. World Intelligence
11. Final Integration

## Runtime cadence

- Daily: adaptive economy, society, governance, crises and intelligence
- Monthly: inflation/prices, career transitions, city development, elections, policy and crisis emergence
- Yearly: demographic transition, generation metrics and historical demographic records

## API

- `GET /api/v1/world/civilization` — complete civilization state
- `GET /api/v1/world/history` — bounded causal world history
- `GET /api/v1/world` — includes Living Civilization summary metrics

## Release rule

The development milestones that were originally described as `v1.2.1` through `v1.3.0` are intentionally consolidated into one release and one tag: **`v1.2.1`**.
