# RedWorld AI v1.2.0 — Human Development to Autonomous Society

`v1.2.0` consolidates the planned Human Development, Community & Culture, Civic Institutions, Collective Behavior, and Autonomous Society capability line into one release tag.

## Human development
Citizens now accumulate learning and career progress, burnout risk, civic engagement, cultural identity, community influence, life-stage state, bounded life-event history, and long-horizon experience. Existing education, skill, physical/mental health, happiness, stress, belonging, autonomy and life satisfaction remain part of the same deterministic profile.

## Community and culture
Community groups evolve cohesion, influence and cultural identity. Citizen belonging and cultural identity feed aggregate cultural vitality.

## Civic institutions
Institutions evolve trust, participation, legitimacy and operational capacity. Education, public health and social mobility are tracked as society-level development indicators.

## Collective behavior
Polarization combines social fragmentation with citizen stress. Inequality pressure responds to social mobility and polarization. When pressure becomes persistent, collective movements can emerge and evolve without bypassing world authority.

## Autonomous society
Collective decisions are generated from civic participation on the deterministic civic cadence and retained in bounded decision history. Decisions and movements are observable through the society API and domain events. The world remains authoritative: these mechanisms produce social state and events, not unrestricted direct mutation of unrelated domains.
