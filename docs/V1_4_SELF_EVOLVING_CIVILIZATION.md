# v1.4.0 - Self-Evolving Civilization

`Agents -> Decisions -> Interactions -> Consequences -> New Conditions -> New Decisions -> History`

The v1.3.1-v1.4.0 line is integrated into the real RedWorld state rather than operating only as an aggregate metrics layer:

- life continuity synchronizes citizen minds and generations with the live population;
- citizen goals and accumulated knowledge feed autonomous-agent goal selection and life profiles;
- social ties synchronize with the real social graph;
- market demand/supply/price signals affect real business prices, wage offers, and productivity;
- public sentiment feeds the existing autonomous society and institutions;
- housing pressure can create connected physical locations in the real geography graph;
- generational transfer updates citizen knowledge/reputation and life-profile education;
- causal records connect pressures to resulting adaptations;
- condition-driven emergent events mutate citizen, city, or civic state;
- civilization adaptation feeds back into society and citizen profiles.

Existing v1.3.0 entity evolution remains authoritative for births, deaths, migration, employment/business creation and closure, policy application, physical development, and crises. Existing Human-in-the-Loop review remains authoritative for high-risk autonomous actions.
