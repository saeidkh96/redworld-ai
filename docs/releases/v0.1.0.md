# RedWorld AI v0.1.0 — First Closed Economy

This release completes the planned development line from v0.0.2 through v0.1.0.

## Implemented milestones
- v0.0.2: double-entry accounting ledger, journal invariants and audit trail
- v0.0.3: citizens, employment contracts, payroll, income/expense state and basic consumption need tracking
- v0.0.4: businesses, production, inventory, sales, revenue/expense accounting and profit calculation
- v0.0.5: registered bank deposits, reserve-backed lending, interest accrual, repayment schedules and bank balance-sheet summary
- v0.0.6: income/business taxation, treasury and welfare spending
- v0.1.0: integrated deterministic closed-economy lifecycle

## Architectural guarantee
New domains are expected to integrate through narrow services, domain events and ledger postings.
The simulation engine orchestrates phases and does not become the owner of every domain rule.

## Known scope boundaries
This release is deterministic and rule-based. Autonomous LLM cognition, memory, social reasoning,
geography, housing, healthcare, education and richer markets are intentionally deferred to later versions.
