# RedWorld AI v1.4.0 - Self-Evolving Civilization

v1.4.0 integrates the v1.3.1-v1.3.9 foundations into a bounded deterministic civilization feedback loop. Citizen intelligence now influences autonomous goals; social dynamics reach the live social graph; market signals affect businesses; institutional feedback reaches society state; urban pressure creates real geography; generational knowledge reaches life profiles; and emergent events/adaptation produce observable world mutations.

High-risk autonomous actions remain under the existing Human-in-the-Loop review path.
